What we make

Not every video needs to sell something. Sometimes you need to train a team, share a patient's story, or help donors understand where their money goes.

  • 01

    Donor and awareness films

    Short documentaries that show donors, members and the public what your organization does, through the people it serves.

    Example
  • 02

    Patient and family stories

    Interviews that feel like conversations, not depositions. Patients, families and staff on camera, in their own words.

    Example
  • 03

    PSAs and TV spots

    Commercials live or die in the first three seconds. Broadcast spots and campaign series, from concept to final delivery.

    Example
  • 04

    Recruitment and testimonials

    Recruitment films, testimonials, training and internal communications for agencies, programs and employers.

Tell us about your project

Josh and Victor

Film school friends. Business partners.

Joshua Land grew up in Baltimore. Victor Fink grew up in Boston. We met in a 16mm filmmaking class at Johns Hopkins and started MindInMotion in 2012.

Every video, whether it's a 30-second spot or a feature film, tells a story. That's how we approach a donor film for a nonprofit and a feature of our own. We work from Baltimore and Orlando.

  • Joshua LandCo-founder. Director, lead editor and colorist.
