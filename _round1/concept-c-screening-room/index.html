What we make

The people in our client films are rarely actors. They’re donor families, patients, researchers, parents and preschoolers. We film them the way we’d want to be filmed.

  1. 1

    Donor and awareness films

    Not every video needs to sell something. Sometimes you need to share a patient’s story, or help donors understand where their money goes.

  2. 2

    Patient and family stories

    Interviews that feel like conversations, not depositions, with patients, families, donors and staff.

  3. 3

    PSAs and TV spots

    Commercials live or die in the first three seconds. We shoot them the way we shoot our features, with motivated lighting, purposeful camera movement and performances that feel real.

  4. 4

    Recruitment and testimonials

    Recruitment films for public agencies and employers, and testimonials that sound like the person saying them.

  5. 5

    Post-production

    Editing, color grading in DaVinci Resolve, VFX and motion graphics, delivery and mastering. We also take on project rescue, when you have footage that still needs to become a film.

How we work

  1. i

    Story first.

    Before we talk about cameras, we want to know who the film is for and what it has to do. Everything else follows from that.

  2. ii

    Collaboration.

    You know your mission and the people you serve. We know how to shoot and cut. We would rather work it out with you than hand over a finished film and hope.

  3. iii

    Craft matters.

    We bring the habits of our features to a two-minute donor film, and we do the edit and the color ourselves.
