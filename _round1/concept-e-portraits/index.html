Tell us about your project

What we do

Most of our work is for organizations whose films are about someone real: a donor family, a patient, a parent, a graduate student. Those people didn't sign up to be on camera. Our job is to make them comfortable enough to be themselves, then light and frame them as carefully as we would in our own films.

Tell us about your project

Conversations, not depositions.

How we work with the people in your film, and with your staff.

  1. 1We start by listening.

    Tell us who the film is for, who should be in it and where it will play. We're a two-person company, so the people on that first call are the ones who shoot and edit your film.

  2. 2We talk with people, not at them.

    A patient or a parent isn't a performer. We want them to sound like themselves, so the interview runs like a conversation.

  3. 3We light people the way we light our films.

    Victor shoots. We bring what we learned making features: motivated lighting, a camera that moves for a reason, performances that feel real.

  4. 4Josh edits and grades it.

    Editing and color in DaVinci Resolve, motion graphics, delivery and mastering. The person who cuts your film also grades it.

What we make

Donor and awareness films, patient and family stories, PSAs and TV spots, recruitment films and testimonials. We also edit, grade and finish projects other people started.
