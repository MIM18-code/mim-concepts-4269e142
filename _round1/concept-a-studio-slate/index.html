Services

What we make

Most of our clients are nonprofits, universities and public agencies. We take a film from the first call to final delivery: planning, directing, camera, edit, color and mastering.

  1. 01

    Donor and awareness films

    Not every video needs to sell something. Sometimes donors need to see where their money goes.

  2. 02

    Patient and family stories

    No scripts and no actors. We work closely with patients, families and their doctors, as we have for the American Lung Association and Infinite Legacy.

  3. 03

    PSAs and TV spots

    Commercials live or die in the first three seconds. We have made TV spots and PSAs for the Maryland State Department of Education and its Maryland EXCELS campaign.

  4. 04

    Recruitment and testimonials

    For police departments and university programs that need people to apply, enroll or sign up.

  5. 05

    Post-production

    Edit, color grading in DaVinci Resolve, motion graphics, delivery and mastering. We also take over projects that stalled somewhere else.

About

Josh and Victor

Film school friends. Business partners. Joshua Land and Victor Fink met in a 16mm filmmaking class at Johns Hopkins and started MindInMotion in 2012.

Josh is a Baltimore native. Victor grew up in Boston. For Johns Hopkins they filmed the Cuba Field Course on location, traveling with the students for the length of the course.
