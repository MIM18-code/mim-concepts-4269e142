# A24 web design research

Captured 2026-09-27. Screenshots are in `research/shots/`, all prefixed `a24-`. Tall pages are sliced in `research/shots/_crops/` (`a24-home-01..04.jpg`, `a24-films-01..04.jpg`, `a24-shop-01..04.jpg`, `a24-shop-backrooms-01.jpg`).

Method: `_tools/shoot-url.js` for the first pass, then a variant (same outputs, plus it strips the cookie and newsletter overlays from the DOM without accepting anything, reports the largest text styles, and adds a mobile full-page JPG). Font names below come from computed styles, not from memory. Hover and menu states were captured with a separate Playwright script. Dead microsites were viewed through web.archive.org snapshots and are labeled "archived".

## The headline finding

The "themed A24 horror site" is mostly a memory. A24's web presence is one strict, neutral system, and the theming comes from the film frames it puts inside that system.

I checked about 40 A24 title domains. What they do today:

- Redirect to the standard a24films.com film page: heretic.movie, bringherback.movie, pearl.movie, warfare.movie, friendship.movie, deathofaunicorn.movie, babygirl.movie, lamb.movie, dreamscenario.movie, priscilla.movie, theironclaw.movie, thezoneofinterest.movie, thebrutalist.movie, adifferentman.movie. Wayback shows midsommar.movie (2019) and men.movie (2022) redirected to a24films.com even during release. In mid and late 2024, heretic.movie and thefrontroom.movie were registrar "future home of" parking pages.
- Redirect to A24 App ticketing: undertone.movie, mothermary.movie, and marty.movie (archived Nov 2025).
- Dead, parked or taken over: x.movie (for sale), maxxxine.movie (redirect spam), y2kmovie.com, martysupreme.com, backrooms.movie (GoDaddy for-sale), beauisafraid.movie (now a gambling spam site), thegreenknight.movie (nginx default), men.movie (empty directory), midsommar.movie and civilwar.movie (hosting placeholders).
- 403 Forbidden: hereditary.movie, isawthetvglow.movie.

Only three bespoke themed sites turned up with evidence: Hereditary (2018), Civil War (2024) and Y2K (2024). Talk to Me (2023) ran on Powster's templated movie platform. So the lesson for MindInMotion is less "build a spooky microsite" and more "build one rigorous, quiet system and let each project's frames and one accent color carry the mood."

## The A24 system in numbers

These values repeat across a24films.com, the films index, the film pages and the shop, so treat them as one type and color system.

- Typefaces: NB International Web for everything, NB International Mono Web for years, prices and counters. Both are licensed Neubau fonts; don't use them. The 2019 site used akzidenz-grotesk and akzidenz-grotesk-extended. The app's type also reports NB International.
- Display: 74px (5.1vw at 1440), weight 500, letter-spacing -0.04em (-2.96px), line-height 0.92. Used for hero titles, section heads ("Upcoming", "All Films") and home feed titles.
- Big list: 120px (8.3vw), weight 500, -0.04em, line-height 0.92. Used only for the back-catalog text list.
- Secondary display: 38px, weight 500, -0.03em, line-height 1.0. Director names, card titles.
- Synopsis: 27px on 36px, weight 400, -0.0125em.
- Labels: 13 to 15px, uppercase, +0.005 to +0.05em, #888 on white or rgba(255,255,255,0.54) on dark. The labels are content types ("SHOP", "PODCAST", "WATCH NOW", "COMING SOON", "WRITTEN AND DIRECTED BY").
- Years: 15px mono, -0.065em, set as a superscript beside the title and top-aligned to the cap height.
- Color: #000, #FFF, #EEE image wells, #888 labels, #C2C2C2 dimmed. Shop background #F1F1F1. No accent color on the main site at all; the stills supply every color.
- Margins: 42px outer margin at 1440 (2.9vw). Film grid columns 432px with 30px gutters.
- Buttons: no pills, no fills. A CTA is a 56px hairline arrow followed by 15 to 21px uppercase text ("SHOP NOW", "LISTEN NOW"). The one boxed control is the "WATCH NOW" dropdown: a 380x62 rectangle with a 1px black border and a chevron.
- Copy tone: short, dry, first person plural. The newsletter blurb promises "Letters from our filmmakers" and emails "not too often". Section names are plain nouns: Films, Television, Docs, Shop, Membership, Notes, App.

## 1. a24films.com home

Screens: a24-home-desk-fold.png, _crops/a24-home-01..04.jpg, a24-home-mob-fold.png, a24-home-menu-open.png.

- Hero, 100vh: a full-bleed film still with no scrim or gradient, and five upcoming titles stacked bottom-left at 74px with 68px line pitch, starting 42px from the left edge. Each title carries a 15px mono year superscript. The hero cycles through the five films and swaps the still; the title of the current film dims to about #9A9A9A while the other four stay white. On the first load the still for "Misty Green" showed out-of-focus green and white verticals, so A24 lets an abstract, nearly illegible frame carry a whole viewport.
- Header over the hero: hamburger plus "MENU" 15px uppercase at top-left, serif logo centered, search icon at top-right, and a thin down arrow at bottom-right. Nothing else.
- Menu (a24-home-menu-open.png): a solid black panel 500px wide slides over the left third of the hero. Seven items at 74px, weight 500, 86px pitch. "CLOSE" with an X at top-left. The hero keeps showing to the right.
- Below the hero is a feed that alternates two row types:
  - Split row: a 746px image well (#EEE for products, a matted B&W photo for podcasts) at x=98, with the text column starting at x=900. The text column has a small uppercase eyebrow ("SHOP", "PODCAST"), a 74px title that wraps as tight as 5 or 6 lines, and a hairline arrow CTA pinned to the bottom of the image.
  - Full-bleed still at 1440x720 (2:1) with a "WATCH NOW" eyebrow and a 74px title bottom-left, 36px in. Again no gradient; they pick frames whose lower-left is already dark or blurred (Onslaught is an extreme close-up of an eye behind yellow glasses, Tony is a motion-blurred crowd).
- Mobile (a24-home-mob-fold.png): one film per screen, title about 48px bottom-left with the mono year under it, and a vertical "1 / 5" counter joined by a 1px line at the right edge.
- On first visit a newsletter modal and a cookie panel covered most of the hero (first capture in this session). That is the worst moment on the site.

## 2. Films index

Screens: _crops/a24-films-01..04.jpg, a24-films-list-hover.png, a24-films-grid-hover.png, a24-films-mob-full.jpg.

- "Upcoming" heading at 74px, then a 3-column masonry of stills in mixed ratios: portrait about 432x508, landscape 432x333, wide 432x283. Each card has "COMING SOON" (15px uppercase grey) above a 38px title. Uneven column bottoms are left alone.
- Grid hover (a24-films-grid-hover.png): the still darkens to about 30% brightness and shows two credit lines at a 24px inset: "RELEASE DATE" label over "Oct 16, 2026", "DIRECTED BY" over the names, 13px labels and 17px values.
- "All Films" heading with "SORT BY: NEWEST / A–Z" at right. The active sort is underlined.
- The newest 12 films get image cards with a mono year above each title. After that the page switches to a text-only list: every title at 120px, one per line, with a mono year superscript. The list runs through the whole back catalog behind "+ SHOW MORE".
- List hover (a24-films-list-hover.png): the hovered title turns grey, and an 840x540 still appears pinned to the right half of the viewport, behind the type. Neighboring titles run over the image. It's the most memorable interaction on the site and it costs nothing: one image per title.
- Mobile: single column, all stills normalized to landscape, title left and mono year or "COMING SOON" right on the same line. The text list continues at about 36px.

## 3. Film pages (Heretic, Bring Her Back, Undertone, Talk to Me)

Screens: a24-heretic-desk-full.jpg, a24-bringherback-desk-full.jpg, a24-undertone-mob-full.jpg, a24-talktome-page-desk-full.jpg.

Horror titles get exactly the same template as comedies and dramas. Heretic, Bring Her Back, Undertone and Talk to Me differ only in the frame they chose.

- Hero at 684px (76vh): full-bleed still, title 74px bottom-left, and a centered outline play triangle about 48x62px with a 1.5px white stroke and no circle behind it. The play glyph is a Lottie animation (the page has `__lottie_element` filters). For Bring Her Back the frame is a bloody hand pressed on rain-streaked glass, for Heretic a man lit by one candle. The horror comes entirely from the frame.
- Credits block on white: a 3-column grid (x = 54, 502, 956). Each cell is a 13px uppercase grey label ("WRITTEN AND DIRECTED BY", "YEAR", "STARRING") over a 38px value. Cast names get one column each. The right column holds "GET TICKETS" with a small arrow and the boxed "WATCH NOW" dropdown.
- A 1px black rule across the full content width, then the synopsis at 27px starting on column 2 (x=502), about 620px wide. Posters sit under it as a pair, 282px each. Then a share row: "SHARE" plus three small glyphs.
- Talk to Me adds a "RELATED PRODUCTS" row: a tee and the "Talk To Me Party Hand", a replica of the film's embalmed hand prop, shot on #EEE.
- Mobile (Undertone): portrait crop of the hero with the title at about 44px and the year under it, the watch dropdown full width, credits stacked, and the synopsis inside an accordion with a chevron.
- 2019 version (a24-midsommar-page-2019-desk-full.jpg): the same grid, set in Akzidenz-Grotesk with a light 64px title, a hero carousel with arrows and dots, and a "RELATED FILMS" row of three horror siblings (Hereditary 2018, The Witch 2016, It Comes At Night 2017) with the year above each title. The template has barely changed in seven years.

## 4. A24 App release pages (Backrooms, The Invite)

Screens: a24-backrooms-app-desk-fold.png, a24-backrooms-app-scroll1.png, a24-invite-app-desk-fold.png.

This is the closest thing A24 has to a per-film theme today, and it is systematic, not bespoke.

- Black page. The top 600px is the film's still blurred (CSS `blur(9px)`), fading to #000. A sharp 288x432 poster card sits on top at left.
- Per-film accent color pulled from the art: Backrooms uses rgb(195,171,61), a mustard from its yellow-wallpaper still. The Invite uses rgb(226,129,151), a pink from its poster title. The accent shows up in only four places: the poster's 1px border, the ticket-icon circle (accent at 7% fill), the location arrow, and the first sentence of the empty state.
- Warm greys, not neutral ones: text #E1DFDE, secondary #B2AFAA, labels #837E77, dividers rgba(48,48,47,0.67).
- Title 64px weight 400 at -0.0475em. Metadata as label/value pairs (12px uppercase labels, 16px values) with year and runtime in mono ("105 mins"). "TRAILER" and "SHARE" are text buttons split by a 1px vertical rule.
- Offer cards (a24-invite-app-desk-fold.png): 432x80 boxes with a 1px border and a circled ticket icon. The member offer's border and text take the accent.
- Empty state as display type: "NO SHOWTIMES FOUND NEAR YOU." in the accent, then "CHOOSE A NEW LOCATION OR REFRESH RESULTS." in grey #6E6A64, all about 64px uppercase with tight leading, and "REFRESH" underlined as the link.
- App 404 (a24-app-404-desk-fold.png): "404" at 943px in the mono face, #353533, with a long extruded shadow gradient running down-right, and "OOPS…" and "PAGE NOT FOUND" at 180px uppercase in #6E6A64 layered over it.
- App landing (a24-app-home-desk-fold.png): "THE APP" at roughly 270px caps, bleeding off the right edge and half hidden behind a phone mockup. A ticket-stub graphic uses red mono "DATE / LOCATION" fields. Membership benefits are a list separated by 1px rules.

## Moves a two-person production company could steal

1. Filmography as a text list with a hover still (a24films.com/films). Set Lotus Eyes, I Like Me, Bad Witch, Ape Canyon, Incubation, Don't Go Hiking and The Farmhouse one per line at about 8vw (120px at 1440), weight 500, tracking -0.04em, line-height 0.92, each with a 15px mono year set as a superscript. On hover or keyboard focus, grey the title to #888 and pin that film's still (bad-witch.webp, vimeo-1173635320.webp, farmhouse-thumbnail.webp) at about 58% width on the right, behind the text. On touch screens show the still inline above each title. Eleven titles is a better length than A24's hundreds.
2. Stacked-title hero that swaps the frame (a24films.com home). Five project titles stacked bottom-left at about 5vw with 0.92 line-height; each one swaps the full-bleed still. Mix the two businesses on purpose: Bad Witch, Checklist Moments, Don't Go Hiking, Ghost Light, Joe. Put a small uppercase label on each ("Feature film, 2018", "Maryland EXCELS"). Dim the active title instead of highlighting it. Stop the auto-advance under `prefers-reduced-motion` and give it a pause control.
3. Pick frames, not scrims (home and film pages). Place type over the part of the frame that is already dark, using `object-position` to put the dark zone bottom-left: bad-witch.webp's forest, Joe-ILF.webp's silhouette, Screen-Shot-2022-06-24-at-6.14.31-PM-2.webp's cold bathroom. Check 4.5:1 contrast and fall back to a small local scrim behind the text block only, never a full-frame gradient.
4. Credit grid as the project-page opener (film pages). Under a 76vh hero, a 3-column grid of 13px uppercase grey labels over 38px values: CLIENT, DIRECTED BY, CINEMATOGRAPHY, YEAR, DELIVERABLES. Then a 1px rule and a 27px synopsis starting on column 2. It works for a Johns Hopkins film and for Bad Witch alike, and it answers "who did what" in five seconds.
5. Thin outline play triangle (film pages). A roughly 48x62px triangle with a 1.5px stroke centered on the still, no circle and no pill, visible without hover. Make the whole hero the `<button>` with a specific name ("Play Infinite Legacy Joe"). It replaces the current white pill.
6. One accent per project, sampled from its own frame (A24 App release pages). Bad Witch: the orange rim light. Don't Go Hiking: blood red. Bennett's Curse: ghoul green. Ghost Light: stage gold. Heart & Thunder: that blue. Use it in four places only: a 1px border on the still, the first clause of one big line, link underlines and the focus ring. Keep everything else neutral. The MindInMotion cyan can be the default when a page has no project.
7. Blurred copy of the still behind the header (A24 App). The same image at `filter: blur(9px)` fills the top 600px and fades to black, with the sharp still as a card on top. No extra asset, and the page takes the color of the film.
8. Warm greys on black (A24 App). Text #E1DFDE, secondary #B2AFAA, labels #837E77 on #000. It reads like a projected print, and it directly answers "blah and dated", since the current site is cold Tailwind grey on #0A0A0A.
9. Empty and utility states as big type (App "NO SHOWTIMES FOUND" and 404). A 404 or form-success page set at about 64px uppercase, first clause in the accent, the rest in warm grey, with one underlined link word. For example: "Nothing here. Tell us about your project instead." The ghost numeral behind it (943px "404" in #353533) can be done with pure CSS.
10. Eyebrow, big title, hairline arrow (home feed rows). The eyebrow is the kind of work in 15px uppercase grey ("COMMERCIAL", "FEATURE FILM", "MUSIC VIDEO"). The title runs at 74px. The CTA is a 56px hairline arrow plus "WATCH" in uppercase. No pill buttons anywhere; the current site is all pills.
11. Masonry that keeps each frame's ratio (films index "Upcoming"). A 3-column grid (432px columns, 30px gutters at 1440) where portrait crops, 16:9 and 2.39 frames coexist and column bottoms don't line up. Show fives-heart-thunder.webp at scope width instead of cropping away its letterbox. Unlike A24, keep title and client visible under every card (audit requirement), and use hover only for the extra credit lines.
12. Mono numerals as a slate (years, prices, runtimes, counters across A24). Put years, durations (":30", "2:14"), and the "1 / 5" hero counter in a free mono such as IBM Plex Mono, JetBrains Mono or Geist Mono at 13 to 15px with slight negative tracking. For a company whose co-founder is an editor and colorist, timecode-looking numbers say something true.
13. Menu as a black panel with display-size items (a24films.com menu). A 500px black panel over the left of the page with Work, Services, Films, About and Contact at about 74px, "CLOSE" at top-left, Escape to close, focus trapped. Five items fit better than A24's seven.
14. Rotating quotes in spaced caps (Hereditary 2018). The four real testimonials in uppercase at about 18 to 21px with 0.14em tracking, names in the accent, crossfading every 6 to 8 seconds, with a visible pause button (auto-updating content needs one) and no rotation under reduced motion. Use a free serif or the site's grotesk, whichever the concept uses.
15. Muted loop with one sound control (Hereditary 2018). The silent reel loop (Vimeo 848454220) full-bleed behind a single-viewport intro, poster still first, CTAs as spaced text links ("Watch the reel", "Tell us about your project"), and one small speaker icon bottom-right that opens the real reel with sound.
16. Related work row (2019 Midsommar page). At the end of each project page, three siblings with the year above the title: Bad Witch leads to Don't Go Hiking, The Farmhouse and Incubation; Maryland EXCELS "Good Mom" leads to the other EXCELS spots and MSDE "Illegal Childcare". It keeps people browsing and doubles as the "CTA where each work section ends" slot with one line of contact below.
17. In-world artifacts from the production (Backrooms shop, Talk to Me "Party Hand", Hereditary "Charlie's Corner"). For each horror title, show one real object from the shoot: DSCF3131.webp (actor in monster makeup), a prop, a call-sheet corner, a location photo. They own all of this. It's the cheapest possible version of A24's diegetic merch and it proves they build things on set.
18. One project per mobile screen with a counter (a24films.com mobile home, Undertone mobile). On phones the hero becomes one full-height frame per project with the title bottom-left, a mono year under it, and a vertical "1 / 5" counter. Project pages put the long synopsis in an accordion under the credits.

## What not to copy

- NB International and NB International Mono. They're licensed Neubau faces. Use a free grotesk with a similar tight, flat-terminal feel (Inter Tight, Geist, Schibsted Grotesk, Instrument Sans) and a free mono.
- The whole A24 skeleton at once: centered serif logo, "MENU" hamburger at left, search at right, stacked 74px titles with superscript years, white credits grid, black footer with uppercase links. Any two of these moves read as influence; all of them read as an A24 skin. Pick a few and give the rest a different shape.
- The centered high-contrast serif monogram as a logo treatment. MindInMotion has its own "M" mark; keep it.
- Title lockups and key art. Hereditary's tree lockup, Y2K's chrome logo and Civil War's lime wordmark are poster-design budgets. MindInMotion has stills, not key art, and og-share.jpg is the only key-art-like frame (the brief says not to caption it with a title).
- Critic pull-quote rotators as critic blurbs. The brief bars festival names and awards, and the testimonials are client quotes. Present them as clients talking, not as a marketing wall of praise.
- The fake operating system (Y2K) and the AI generator (Civil War). These are one-off campaign builds with logins, playlists and a chatbot or image model behind them. They also expire: y2k.movie now just redirects.
- "Experience only on mobile" (Civil War). Most of MindInMotion's buyers are marketing and comms staff on work desktops.
- Countdown clocks (Y2K). Only useful with a real, confirmed date. The brief has none for Incubation.
- Newsletter modals, cookie panels and pinned promos over the content (a24films.com first load, the shop's "YOU'VE GOT CREDITS" box). A24 can afford to annoy people; a vendor pitching a nonprofit can't.
- Retailer logo grids and ticketing tabs (Talk to Me on Powster). Where Bad Witch or Lotus Eyes stream is one line of text with links, not a tile wall.
- Grey #EEE product wells and floating product shots (home feed, shop). They exist to show merch. MindInMotion has nothing to sell and should give that space to frames.
- Hover-only credits (films index grid). A24 hides release date and director until hover. The audit requires title and client visible on every tile without hover.
- A separate domain per film. A24's own .movie domains show what happens after release: redirects at best, and at worst parked pages or spam (beauisafraid.movie now serves a gambling site; x.movie and maxxxine.movie are gone). Keep every film on mindinmotion.tv under /films/.
- Giant type as filler. A24's 120px list works because the titles are the content. A 120px "What we do" heading over a paragraph is the stock-template habit this redesign is trying to lose.
