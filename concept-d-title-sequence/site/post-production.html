Our Services

Based in Baltimore, we’ve built our editing and color capabilities over a decade of feature films and commercial work. Whether you need a full post workflow or help with a specific piece—color, VFX—we can take your project across the finish line.

  1. 01

    Editing

    Story-driven editing from editors who've cut features, commercials, and everything in between. We work in DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, Premiere, and Avid—whatever your workflow requires.

  2. 02

    Color Grading

    Professional color work in DaVinci Resolve. From technical corrections to establishing a cinematic look, we handle everything from LOG footage to final delivery.

  3. 03

    VFX & Motion Graphics

    Compositing, cleanup, and motion graphics. We partner with trusted specialists for complex VFX work while handling standard effects in-house.

  4. 04

    Delivery & Mastering

    Final files in any format—broadcast specs, streaming platforms, social media, or theatrical DCPs. We handle the technical so you don't have to.

  5. 05

    Project Rescue

    Stalled project? Previous editor unavailable? We regularly take over in-progress work and get it to the finish line.

SC 03

Collaboration Is the Point

We've built our post-production process around collaboration. You'll have direct access to your editor throughout the project. We share cuts early and often, incorporate feedback quickly, and keep you in the loop at every stage.

Our workflow is built for revision. We know that the best work comes from iteration, so we’ve structured our process to make changes easy rather than painful.

Whether you’re in Baltimore or across the country, we use secure remote review tools that make collaboration simple. Watch cuts together in real-time, leave timestamped notes, and approve final versions—all without shipping hard drives.
