Common Questions

How long does a typical project take?
Timeline varies by scope. A simple testimonial video might be delivered in 1-2 weeks, while a full commercial campaign typically takes 4-8 weeks from kickoff to final delivery.
Do you travel for productions?
Yes. We're based in Baltimore, and we regularly travel throughout the Mid-Atlantic, Southeast, and beyond. Travel costs are included in project quotes.
What's included in the quoted price?
Our quotes include pre-production planning, equipment, crew, production, editing, color grading, motion graphics, and final delivery. We'll clearly outline any additional costs upfront.
How do revisions work?
Each project includes a set number of revision rounds. We present rough cuts for feedback and refine until you're happy with the result. Additional revisions can be added if needed.
Can you match our existing brand guidelines?
Yes. We work with your brand assets, color palettes, fonts, and style guides so the videos match the rest of your marketing.
