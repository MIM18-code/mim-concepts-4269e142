## Kept as-is, but worth a look

These are live copy, so they stay unless you say otherwise. Most are claims the content brief or the September audit marked as unconfirmed, or facts that don't match from page to page.

1. "five feature films" (Film intro, About hero) and "Five features and counting" (About). The brief says the count may be higher.
2. "screened at festivals across the globe and secured worldwide distribution" (Film intro).
3. "dozens of nonprofits and foundations" (Corporate).
4. "We respond within 24 hours" (Contact) and "Josh or Victor replies within 24 hours" (homepage closing card, now on every page). The brief says one business day at most. This one is still open from the homepage.
5. About's film row marks Incubation "In Post" under "Five features and counting—available on all major VOD platforms", while the About story and both bios say Incubation is "currently screening at festivals".
6. Which features screened: About says "Lotus Eyes" and "I Like Me" have screened at festivals and stream on VOD. The homepage names "I Like Me" and "Bad Witch".
7. The Cuba film has four names and two clients: "Cuba Field Course Documentary" for Johns Hopkins SAIS (Portfolio case study), "Cuba, The Field Course Experience" for Johns Hopkins Advanced Academic Programs (Portfolio grid), "Cuba Field Course" (Post) and "Cuba Field Course Experience" for Johns Hopkins AAP (homepage).
8. Director credits on the Film page mix "Director: Joshua Land", "Directors: Joshua Land & Victor Fink" and "Director: Josh Land & Victor Fink". Each is verbatim from the Portfolio.
9. The client is "Maryland State Department of Education" on the inner pages, "MD State Dept of Education" on the homepage and "MD State Education" in the About client list.
10. The homepage hero still shows "Clients served 100+", which the inner pages leave out as unconfirmed.
11. The Contact FAQ says "the Mid-Atlantic, Southeast, and beyond". The Southeast presence was Orlando.
12. "Rising Tide Innovation Center, St. Petersburg FL" in the Portfolio and Corporate grids. It's a client in Florida, not the Orlando office, so I left it in.
13. The FAQ promises "1-2 weeks" for a testimonial, "4-8 weeks" for a campaign, travel costs included, and "a set number of revision rounds". The audit listed revision policy as unconfirmed.
14. The photo of Josh and Victor on stage (About, and alt text on Film) is described as a festival screening Q&A. Who's in it and where it was taken is still open from the audit.
