## Kept as-is, but worth a look

These are live copy, so they stay unless you say otherwise. Each is a claim the content brief or the September audit marked as unconfirmed.

1. "five feature films" (Film intro, About hero) and "Five features and counting" (About). The brief says the count may be higher.
2. "screened at festivals across the globe and secured worldwide distribution" (Film intro).
3. "dozens of nonprofits and foundations" (Corporate).
4. "We respond within 24 hours" (Contact) and "Josh or Victor replies within 24 hours" (homepage closing card). This one is still open from the homepage.
5. The Cuba case study says "Johns Hopkins SAIS". Every other mention says "Johns Hopkins Advanced Academic Programs".
6. "Rising Tide Innovation Center, St. Petersburg FL" in the Portfolio grid. It's a client in Florida, not the Orlando office. I left it in.
7. The FAQ promises: "1-2 weeks" for a testimonial, "4-8 weeks" for a campaign, travel costs included, and "a set number of revision rounds". The audit listed revision policy as unconfirmed.
