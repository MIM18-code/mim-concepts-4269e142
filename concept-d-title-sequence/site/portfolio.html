Case 01Maryland State Department of Education

Maryland State Department of Education

Maryland Excels Campaign

The Maryland State Department of Education needed videos that would reach parents across the state and convince them to choose licensed, quality childcare. The challenge: government messaging that doesn't feel like government messaging.

We took a documentary approach. Instead of actors, we filmed real families at real childcare centers throughout Maryland. We followed parents through their actual routines, and captured kids being kids. The result was a multi-video campaign that aired statewide and drove traffic to the Maryland Excels program.

Deliverables
TV spots, digital campaign, social cuts

Case 02Infinite Legacy

Infinite Legacy

Organ Donation Testimonials

How do you tell stories about loss, grief, and hope without exploiting any of it? That was the question when Infinite Legacy asked us to create testimonial videos featuring donor families and organ recipients.

We approached each interview like a conversation, not a production. Small crew, intimate setups, no rush. We let people share their stories in their own words, on their own timeline. The editing focused on honoring those stories rather than manipulating emotion. These videos have been used at fundraising galas, in hospital partner communications, and across the organization's awareness campaigns.

Deliverables
Testimonial series, gala content, awareness campaign

Case 03Johns Hopkins SAIS

Johns Hopkins SAIS

Cuba Field Course Documentary

Johns Hopkins School of Advanced International Studies needed to capture their Cuba field course for program recruitment. The logistics were complicated: international travel, limited crew, working around academic schedules, and navigating Cuban filming restrictions.

We embedded with the students for the duration of the course. A small crew meant we could move fast—classroom discussions, street interviews, cultural experiences—without disrupting the academic program. The final documentary has been used by Hopkins for years to recruit students into their international studies programs.

Deliverables
Documentary, recruitment content, program materials

Case 04American Lung Association

American Lung Association

Lung Cancer Patient Stories

The American Lung Association asked us to tell the stories of lung cancer patients and their healthcare providers, and to explain biomarker testing along the way.

We worked closely with patients and doctors. Sydney's story shows how biomarker testing changed her prognosis. Ashley and Dr. Reed's video explores the patient-provider relationship during diagnosis. The campaign has been used nationally by the American Lung Association for patient education and advocacy.

Deliverables
Patient testimonial series, provider education content, advocacy campaign
