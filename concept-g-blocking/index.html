    Since 2012

    Making movies and videos for clients who care about quality

    MindInMotion was founded by Baltimore native Joshua Land, and Boston transplant Victor Fink in 2012. We met in a 16mm filmmaking class in college and quickly bonded over being obsessed with movies and wanting to spend as much time outside of school working on film projects as possible.

    Since then we've made five feature films together, and grown a company making film and video for a wide range of clients. Our work spans the realms of documentary and fiction, and has taken us everywhere from Baltimore, Washington DC, and Orlando, Florida to the mountains of Washington state and the rainforests of Cuba.

    Our feature films "I Like Me" and "Bad Witch" have screened at festivals across the world and are available to view on all major VOD platforms. When we're not working for clients, we're working on our own projects. Our new feature film, "Incubation", is now screening at festivals.
