About MindInMotion

Baltimore native Joshua Land and Boston transplant Victor Fink founded MindInMotion in 2012. We met in a 16mm filmmaking class at Johns Hopkins.

Since then we've made our own feature films together and grown a company that makes film and video for clients. We've shot documentary and fiction in Baltimore, Washington DC, the mountains of Washington state and the rainforests of Cuba.

Our feature films "I Like Me" and "Bad Witch" have screened at festivals and are available on major VOD platforms. Our new feature film, "Incubation," is now screening at festivals.
