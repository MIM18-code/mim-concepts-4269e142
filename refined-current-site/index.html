Behind the Scenes

About MindInMotion

MindInMotion was founded by Baltimore native Joshua Land and Boston transplant Victor Fink in 2012. We met in a 16mm filmmaking class in college.

Since then we've made our own feature films together and grown a company that makes film and video for clients. We've shot documentary and fiction in Baltimore, Washington DC, the mountains of Washington state and the rainforests of Cuba.

Our feature films "I Like Me" and "Bad Witch" have screened at festivals across the world and are available to view on all major VOD platforms. Our new feature film, "Incubation", is now screening at festivals.
