Our Story

Joshua Land and Victor Fink met in a 16mm filmmaking class at Johns Hopkins University and have been collaborating ever since. Josh grew up in Baltimore; Victor in Boston. Together they co-founded MindInMotion, a video production company based in Baltimore.

Over the past decade-plus, they’ve produced features, shorts, music videos, and commercials together. Their features include "Lotus Eyes," "I Like Me," "Bad Witch," "Ape Canyon" and their latest, "Incubation."

Alongside their own films, they make video for nonprofits, universities, public agencies and brands. They've shot documentary and fiction in Baltimore, Washington DC, the mountains of Washington state and the rainforests of Cuba.

Their feature films have screened at festivals across the world, and most are available on all major VOD platforms. "Incubation" is currently screening at festivals.

How We Work

  • Documentary Interviews

    Much of our client work is documentary. We interview the people involved and build the edit around what they say.

  • Working With Josh and Victor

    You talk to us directly. We ask questions, offer ideas, and say so when we think something could be better.

  • Post-Production

    We edit and color grade what we shoot, and work with sound-mixing partners for the final mix.
