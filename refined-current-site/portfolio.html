Featured Projects

Maryland Excels Campaign

Maryland State Department of Education

The Maryland State Department of Education needed videos that would reach parents across the state and convince them to choose licensed, quality childcare, without sounding like government ads.

We took a documentary approach and filmed families at childcare centers around Maryland instead of casting actors. We followed parents through their daily routines. The result was a multi-video campaign that aired statewide and drove traffic to the Maryland Excels program.

Deliverables
TV spots, digital campaign, social cuts

Organ Donation Testimonials

Infinite Legacy

Infinite Legacy asked us to film donor families and organ recipients talking about loss, grief and hope, without exploiting their stories.

We filmed each interview with a small crew and no time limit. These videos have been used at fundraising galas, in hospital partner communications, and across the organization's awareness campaigns.

Deliverables
Testimonial series, gala content, awareness campaign

Cuba Field Course Documentary

Johns Hopkins Advanced Academic Programs

Johns Hopkins Advanced Academic Programs needed to capture their Cuba field course for program recruitment. The logistics were complicated: international travel, limited crew, working around academic schedules, and navigating Cuban filming restrictions.

We traveled with the students for the whole course. With a small crew we could film classroom discussions and street interviews without disrupting the program. Hopkins has used the documentary for years to recruit students into its international programs.

Deliverables
Documentary, recruitment content, program materials

Lung Cancer Patient Stories

American Lung Association

The American Lung Association asked us to tell the stories of lung cancer patients and their healthcare providers, and to explain biomarker testing along the way.

Sydney's story shows how biomarker testing changed her prognosis. Ashley and Dr. Reed talk about the patient-provider relationship during diagnosis. The American Lung Association has used the series nationally for patient education and advocacy.

Deliverables
Patient testimonial series, provider education content, advocacy campaign
