Our Services

We're based in Baltimore and have edited and colored feature films and commercials for more than a decade. We can run the full post workflow or take on one piece of it, like color or VFX.

  • Editing

    Our editors have cut feature films, commercials, documentaries and corporate videos. We work in DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, Premiere, and Avid.

  • Color Grading

    Color correction and grading in DaVinci Resolve. We work from LOG camera footage through to the graded master.

  • VFX & Motion Graphics

    Compositing, cleanup, and motion graphics. We do standard effects in-house and bring in VFX specialists for complex shots.

  • Delivery & Mastering

    Final files to broadcast specs, for streaming platforms and social media, or as theatrical DCPs.

  • Project Rescue

    We take over projects that have stalled or lost their editor, and finish them.
