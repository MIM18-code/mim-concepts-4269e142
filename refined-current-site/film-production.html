Production and post-production support for features, shorts, documentaries, and music videos.

MindInMotion is a full service film production company in Maryland based in Baltimore. We have extensive experience with the entire process of producing and finishing shorts and feature films, and our own features have screened at festivals across the globe; most are on major VOD platforms.

If you're looking to get your next film project started and you don't know where to begin, you're not alone. We've been there. Making a film is rarely a simple, easy, or straightforward process, and although every project is different, our years of experience working on a multitude of film projects in various capacities has helped us learn how to make the process simpler and more manageable.

To say we love making films is an understatement. It's a difficult process, but it's always rewarding. We continuously strive to be one of the best film production companies in Baltimore, as well as up and down the east coast, and actively try to improve with each project. We promise to always be upfront and honest about every step of the process of completing the film, and help you determine what is possible and how the most can be achieved for any budget. If you're looking for help getting your next film off the ground, or you're looking to fill specific production roles, fill out the contact form and we'll let you know how we can help.
